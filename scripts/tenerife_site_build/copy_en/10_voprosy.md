# Questions before entering Artemis Tenerife, and straight answers

Here are the questions people ask before buying a home in Tenerife and before buying shares in the Artemis developer company. The answers are short and come with figures, just as we would give them face to face.

[FRAME F03] <!-- internal marker, not published -->

### Location

**Which area?** La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, on the northern slope of Tenerife, exit 29 off the TF-5 motorway. We choose the exact site together with queue members and put it to a majority vote.

**How far to the sea, the town and the airport?** Playa del Ancón is about 3 km. Puerto de la Cruz is 8 km, 10–15 minutes by car. Tenerife North airport is 15–20 minutes, Santa Cruz de Tenerife 25–30 minutes, and Tenerife South airport about an hour.

**Why not the south?** People holiday in the south for a week; in the north, they live. The north is green, not too hot and not dusty, with towns where life goes on all year round. That is exactly what you need for long-term rental and for living there yourself.

**What about winter?** Evenings in the north can be cool. That is why the homes have deep windows, a stone shell and smart climate control, and the shared pool is heated.

### What I'm buying

**What are developer company shares?** A way into the company that will build the twenty Artemis residences. One share equals one square metre of the home's total built area, excluding common areas.

**How does it work?** Joining the queue is free. We are now choosing the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, and discussing the options with queue members. We take no money before the site is chosen: the investment contract arrives once most of the queue approves the site. Under the contract you buy developer company shares at the price of the current stage: one share equals one m² of the home's total built area, excluding common areas. Pay in full upfront for a 10% discount on the entry price, in instalments over six months — six equal payments with a 5% discount — or in instalments over a year with no discount. A report every week of construction. Once the home is completed, you receive a dividend in euros on every share from long-term rental after costs, estimated and before tax, or exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence.

**How is a share different from a home?** You buy a home at completion, finished. You buy a share earlier, at the stage price: ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion. A share gives you a choice after completion: dividend or metres.

**What is the price now and at the end?** A metre as a share costs ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion — an estimate based on current prices of comparable new apartments in the area. Entry is cheaper at the early stages, and the price is planned to rise towards completion.

### Price and early entry

**Why does early entry cost less?** The share price is planned to rise with the construction stages: queue, permit, foundations, structure and roof, finishing, completion. With each completed stage the project's risk will fall and the metre will cost more. Early entry is the price of entering the company, not a discount on a finished home.

**Can I pay in instalments?** Yes. Over six months: six equal payments with no down payment, the first on signing the contract, a 5% discount on the whole price. Over a year: no discount, monthly payment = entry price ÷ 12. In full upfront: a 10% discount on the entry price.

**When do I pay?** Only after most of the queue has approved the site and you have signed the investment contract. You pay nothing when you join.

### Dividend

**How does the dividend work?** The dividend is paid in euros on every share and is the same for all shares. It is calculated from long-term rental after costs: a year's rent per metre minus 46.3% in itemised costs — IBI property tax, community fees, management, vacancy, insurance, repair reserve, rubbish collection charge. At the queue entry price, the expected dividend is 2.5–3.5% a year before tax.

**What does that mean for 72 shares?** If you buy 72 shares at the queue entry price, the expected dividend is 2.5–3.5% a year before tax.

**Where does the rent figure come from?** From the market: long-term rent in Santa Úrsula is about 12 € per metre, about 15 € for apartments, and about 17 € in Puerto de la Cruz.

**Why only long-term rental?** Canary Islands law Ley 6/2025 rules out short-term holiday lets in new builds. So the Artemis dividend is calculated only from long-term rental.

**Why is the dividend shown as a range?** Future rent can only be estimated, so the dividend is shown as a range too: an expected dividend of 2.5–3.5% a year before tax at the queue entry price. The euros per share are the same for everyone, but the percentage on what you invest depends on the stage at which you enter: at later stages the entry price is higher and the percentage lower.

This is a forecast, not a guarantee: the dividend may be lower or may not be paid, and the share price may fall. Tax consequences depend on your personal situation.

### Metres

**How do I exchange shares for metres?** After the occupancy permit: a deed of sale at the notary for your residence and registration in the property register. 72 shares become 72 metres. Taxes on the transaction follow the laws of Spain and the Canary Islands; details are in the terms and on request.

**Can I buy more shares?** Yes, at the price of the current stage. Started with 50 shares and want 72 metres? Buy 22 more.

**What can I do with my shares after completion?** Two paths: receive a dividend in euros on every share, or exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence.

**Will you help with a mortgage?** Yes. A mortgage is available only to a buyer, but an investor can pay a first instalment of 50% from their own funds and cover the rest with a mortgage once the building permit is granted. Our brokers help you get a mortgage from banks in Spain and other European countries.

**Can I sell my unit before the keys?** If there turn out to be more buyers than units, we will offer investors the chance to pass their unit to a buyer at the price per square metre in effect at that moment. This is an opportunity, not a promise of income.

### Construction and reports

**When are construction and the keys?** Start: planned for early 2027. Completion: planned for late 2028 to mid-2029.

**Who will see the reports?** Shareholders and home buyers. The report will go out every week: the stage, what was done, what comes next.

**How can I follow the site from abroad?** Through the weekly report: the stage, what was done that week, what comes next.

**What will I get at completion?** A new build, not someone else's renovation: a stone shell, solar panels flush with the roof, smart climate and lighting, a shared heated pool.
