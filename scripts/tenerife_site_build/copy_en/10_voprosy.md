# Questions about Artemis Tenerife, and straight answers

Here are the questions people ask before buying a home in Tenerife and before joining the Artemis interest list. The answers are short and come with figures, just as we would give them face to face.

[FRAME F03] <!-- internal marker, not published -->

### Location

**Which area?** La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, on the northern slope of Tenerife, exit 29 off the TF-5 motorway. We are selecting the exact site.

**How far to the sea, the town and the airport?** Playa del Ancón is about 3 km. Puerto de la Cruz is 8 km, 10–15 minutes by car. Tenerife North airport is 15–20 minutes, Santa Cruz de Tenerife 25–30 minutes, and Tenerife South airport about an hour.

**Why not the south?** People holiday in the south for a week; in the north, they live. The north is green, not too hot and not dusty, with towns where life goes on all year round.

**What about winter?** Evenings in the north can be cool. That is why the homes have deep windows, a stone shell and smart climate control, and the shared pool is heated.

[FRAME F01] <!-- internal marker, not published -->

### The project

**What is Artemis Tenerife?** 20 residences planned in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula. Concept stage: we are selecting the site.

**How is it financed?** Financing: founders' capital, a bank development loan and a small group of private co-investors. Home sales open after the building permit.

**Can I take part as a co-investor?** Co-investment from €200,000, individually. Details are discussed in person after a call.

**What does joining the list commit me to?** Nothing. It is a free, non-binding interest list. No payment, no contract. Joining the list creates no contract and no investment.

### Price

**What will a home cost?** Home prices will be published once the site and building permit are confirmed. Market reference: neighbouring La Quinta new builds 3,360–3,870 €/m² (asking, sold out); plus resale La Quinta 2,812 €/m² (Fotocasa, October 2026).

**When can I buy a home?** Home sales open after the building permit.

**How will payment work?** Prices and payment terms are provided individually.

### The homes

**How many homes, and what kind?** Twenty: six one-bedroom homes of 48–55 m², ten two-bedroom homes of 68–78 m² and four large homes of 85–100 m².

**What will I get at completion?** A new build, not someone else's renovation: a stone shell, solar panels flush with the roof, smart climate and lighting, a shared heated pool.

### Construction and reports

**When are construction and the keys?** Start: planned for early 2027. Completion: planned for late 2028 to mid-2029.

**Who will see the reports?** Home buyers. The report will go out every week: the stage, what was done, what comes next.

**How can I follow the site from abroad?** Through the weekly report: the stage, what was done that week, what comes next.
