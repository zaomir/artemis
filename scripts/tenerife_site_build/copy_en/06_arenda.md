# A home to use in your own way

Live year-round, spend winters here or let it long-term — the residence owner decides. The Artemis investment model ends in a cash exit and does not provide annual rental distributions.

[КАДР F10]

### For living

A bright living room, terrace, smart climate and heated pool. Choose your size: [48 to 100 m²](/tenerife/producto/).

[КАДР F04]

### For long-term letting

Layout, running costs and access to town matter for a long stay. A letting calculation includes home maintenance, repairs and periods between tenants.

[КАДР F05]

### For investors

The investor pays for participation once and receives a cash settlement on sale or buyback at exit. The model targets @target@% annualised over @months@ months. [Investment model](/tenerife/inversores/).
