# Your investment, target income and exit proceeds

Choose a budget to see one upfront payment and the target result after @months@ months. The model uses @target@% annualised with compounding; capital and income are paid together on sale or buyback. There are no annual distributions.

[КАДР F20]

### Your inputs

The amount changes in @entry@ € increments, the price of one share. Calculator range: 48–100 shares, from @entry_48@ € to @entry_100@ €. The share quantity sets your participation size; it does not reserve a home.

### Your results

- **Investment** — one payment at entry.
- **Exit proceeds** — capital and target income together.
- **Total income** — proceeds less investment.
- **Target return** — @target@% annualised.
- **Total increase** — @gain_pct@% over three years.
- **Source of settlement** — a sale or buyback funded by home-sale receipts.

### Formulas

Investment = shares × @entry@ €. Exit proceeds = investment × (1 + @target@%)³. Income = proceeds − investment. The @growth@% house-price growth assumption feeds the sales plan; it is not added to the investor's @target@% return.

### Where the exit money comes from

Payments from buyers of twenty residences. In the base scenario these cover the budget and full investor settlement, without additional developer capital. [Funding sources](/tenerife/inversores/#funding).

### What affects the calculation

@market@ €/m² is a provisional comparison reference, @growth@% is an annual growth assumption and @target@% annualised is a target. Sales, costs and actual timing can change the payment. [Assumptions and sensitivity](/tenerife/inversores/#assumptions).
