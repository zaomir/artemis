# Artemis Tenerife terms: home types, built area, individual prices

Here you'll find the basic terms of Artemis Tenerife: what homes are planned, what counts as built area, how prices will be published and how financing works. 20 residences planned in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula. Concept stage: we are selecting the site. Prices and payment terms are provided individually.

[FRAME F20] <!-- internal marker, not published -->

### Home types and built area

The complex has twenty homes in three sizes.

| Type | Of 20 | Built area |
|---|---|---|
| One bedroom | 6 | 48–55 m² |
| Two bedrooms | 10 | 68–78 m² |
| Large home | 4 | 85–100 m² |

1,308–1,510 m² in total across the twenty residences. Area means a home's total built area — construida in Spanish — excluding common areas. The shared pool and the grounds around the homes are not part of a home's area.

### Prices

Home prices will be published once the site and building permit are confirmed. Market reference: neighbouring La Quinta new builds 3,360–3,870 €/m² (asking, sold out); plus resale La Quinta 2,812 €/m² (Fotocasa, October 2026).

Prices and payment terms are provided individually.

[FRAME F07] <!-- internal marker, not published -->

### Financing

Financing: founders' capital, a bank development loan and a small group of private co-investors. Home sales open after the building permit.

[FRAME F11] <!-- internal marker, not published -->

### Co-investment

Co-investment from €200,000, individually. Details are discussed in person after a call. Joining the list creates no contract and no investment.

[FRAME F17] <!-- internal marker, not published -->

### What happens next

We are selecting the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula. Once the site and the building permit are confirmed, home prices will be published. Construction is planned to start in early 2027, with completion planned for late 2028 to mid-2029.
