# One share, one metre: Artemis developer company shares

Shares in the Artemis Tenerife developer company are sold at the price of the construction stage: one share equals one square metre of the home's total built area, excluding common areas. The price per metre is ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion — an estimate based on current prices of comparable new apartments in the area. You pay once most of the queue has approved the site and the investment contract has arrived: in full upfront with a 10% discount on the entry price, over six months in six equal payments with a 5% discount, or over a year with no discount. After completion: a dividend in euros on every share, or exchange your shares for metres.

### Why shares, not a home

A buyer of a finished home pays for a metre that already has a permit, foundations, structure, roof, finishes and a pool. A shareholder comes in earlier, before any of that exists, and pays the price of the stage the project has reached.

Early entry is not a discount on a finished home. It is the price of entering the company at the start. A finished metre costs more because completed work stands behind it. You pay for the metre earlier and hold the share while that work takes shape.

### How it works

1. **The queue.** Joining is free. You don't need to pay anything now.
2. **Site and contract.** We choose the site, most of the queue approves it, you receive the investment contract and buy shares at the price of the current stage: one share equals one metre.
3. **Payment.** In full upfront with a 10% discount on the entry price, in instalments over six months — six equal payments with a 5% discount — or in instalments over a year with no discount.
4. **A weekly report.** Shareholders and home buyers will see which stage construction has reached.
5. **After completion.** A dividend in euros on every share from long-term rental after costs, or exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence.

### Six price stages

| Stage | What has to happen |
|---|---|
| Queue | site approved by most of the queue, investment contract |
| Permit | building licence granted |
| Foundations | base and stone plinth complete |
| Structure and roof | walls, floors, roof with panels |
| Finishing | façade, services, interiors, pool |
| Completion | occupancy permit |

Key project dates, as planned: construction starts in early 2027; completion in late 2028 to mid-2029.

The stage will change when something happens on site, not when a date arrives. You will see every event in the weekly report.

The stage price is the price the company sets for its share at that stage. As a guide: ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion — an estimate based on current prices of comparable new apartments in the area. For comparison, the asking prices at the neighbouring La Quinta new builds are 3,360–3,870 € per metre; both projects have already sold out.

### Working out a purchase of 72 metres

A two-bedroom home of 72 metres equals 72 shares.

- **Entry price** = 72 shares × the price per metre at your stage, ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion.
- **In full upfront**: a 10% discount on the entry price.
- **Over six months**: six equal payments with no down payment, the first on signing the contract, a 5% discount on the whole price; each payment = entry price × 0.95 ÷ 6.
- **Over a year**: no discount, monthly payment = entry price ÷ 12.

### Dividend: the same euros on every share

Shares not exchanged for metres after completion stay in the company. The company lets those metres on long-term leases. The dividend is the rent from one metre after costs, before tax, per share. That is why it is the same for every share, whenever it was bought.

**How it is calculated.** Long-term rent in Santa Úrsula is 12–15 € per metre a month. Itemised costs come to 46.3% of rent: IBI property tax 2.3%, community fees 21.0%, rental management 10.7% (incl. IGIC), vacancy 4.2%, insurance 2.2%, repair reserve 5.0%, rubbish collection charge 0.9%. The remainder is divided equally between the shares. Hence an **expected dividend of 2.5–3.5% a year before tax at the queue entry price**.

This is a forecast, not a guarantee: the dividend may be lower or may not be paid, and the share price may fall. Tax consequences depend on your personal situation.

### What that means against what you put in

The dividend in euros is the same, but the entry price depends on the stage. So early entry gives a higher percentage on what you invested. Estimated on current long-term rents in the area, at the queue entry price the expected dividend is 2.5–3.5% a year before tax; at later stages it is lower.

For comparison: ten-year German Bunds stood at 3.34% on 30 October 2026, the best one-year fixed-term deposits (Festgeld) averaged 3.52%, and gross rental yield in Germany's 52 largest cities is 4.17%.

### Exchanging shares for metres

After the occupancy permit, you can exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence. 72 shares become 72 metres, a two-bedroom home. The exchange is completed with a deed of sale at the notary and registration in the property register. Taxes on the transaction follow the laws of Spain and the Canary Islands; details are in the terms and on request.

### Buying more

Started with 50 shares and want a 72-metre home? Buy 22 more shares at the price of the current stage.

**After completion.** Every share has two paths: a dividend in euros from long-term rental after costs, or exchange for the metres of your own residence. Shares you buy later follow the same rules.

### Mortgage

A mortgage is available only to a buyer, but an investor can pay a first instalment of 50% from their own funds and cover the rest with a mortgage once the building permit is granted. Our brokers help you get a mortgage from banks in Spain and other European countries.

### Where the construction money comes from

Before construction, investors' money covers the team, design and permits. Construction itself is funded by developer company shares and home sales. Each stage is a phase that will rise on site. **Simon Subic** links the money to the construction, and **Boris Ribaut** will run the site.

### Passing your unit to a buyer

If there turn out to be more buyers than units, we will offer investors the chance to pass their unit to a buyer at the price per square metre in effect at that moment. This is an opportunity, not a promise of income.
