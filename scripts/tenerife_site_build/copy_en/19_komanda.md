# The Artemis de la Cruz team

Different team members handle project preparation and work on site. This page explains their responsibilities; their individual profiles hold the detailed experience and portfolio.

[КАДР F22]

### Simon Subic — Developer

Plot selection, permits, launch and linking finance to construction stages. Simon works between London and Paris and answers questions when a report differs from what is observed on site. [Simon Subic’s detailed profile](/tenerife/simon/).

### Boris Ribaut — Supervisor

The site, structure, contractors, reports and handover. Boris manages the technical work: drawings, materials, building services and checks of completed work. [Boris Ribaut’s detailed profile](/tenerife/boris/).

### How the work is organised

The developer handles plot selection and preparation of documents; the lead builder is responsible for work on site. Participants see their combined work in the weekly report. [Project progress](/tenerife/construccion/), [transaction process and documents](/tenerife/proceso/).
