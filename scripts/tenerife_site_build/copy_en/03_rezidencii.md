### What all twenty share

**Outside: Canary Stone.**
- Warm lime render: a light façade that ages gracefully in the sun.
- A plinth of dark volcanic stone: the island's own material.
- A flat roof with a slim bronze edge and dark solar panels set flush into the roof.
- Tall windows in slim bronze frames.
- Stone terraces with bronze handrails and glass.

**Inside.** An open-plan room opening onto the terrace, light walls, stone and bronze details. Smart climate and lighting. Deep windows and a stone shell reduce heat loss.

**A shared heated pool.** Evenings in the north can be cool, and heating keeps the pool usable all year round.
