# I'm Simon Subic, developer of Artemis Tenerife

I'm a Development Principal working between London and Paris. On Artemis Tenerife — 20 residences planned in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, on the northern slope of Tenerife — I lead site selection, permits, launch and linking the money to the construction. Concept stage: we are selecting the site. Start: planned for early 2027. Completion: planned for late 2028 to mid-2029.

[FRAME F19] <!-- internal marker, not published -->

### What I do

I run development projects on the owner's side: from choosing the asset to the moment the home is up and running. My chain looks like this:

**Project criteria → site search → asset due diligence → deal structure → permits → capital → development platform → construction launch → operation or exit.**

Within this chain I do three things:

- **Choosing and checking the asset.** Is the site right, does the economics hold up, where are the risks.
- **Capital, permits and stakeholders.** How to connect the money, the permits and everyone who influences the project.
- **Platform and construction launch.** The team, controls and procedures without which construction never gets onto the site.

I'm a Slovenian citizen, working on a London-based platform and in France.

### Why La Quinta

The north of Tenerife is green, not too hot, not dusty. From La Quinta it is 8 km to Puerto de la Cruz, 10–15 minutes by car; 15–20 minutes to Tenerife North airport; 25–30 minutes to Santa Cruz. People live here all year round. I build where people live, not where they spend a week.

Twenty homes: six one-bedroom homes of 48–55 m², ten two-bedroom homes of 68–78 m² and four large homes of 85–100 m². An intimate complex, not a block of hundreds of flats. For comparison, the neighbouring La Quinta new builds were offered at 3,360–3,870 €/m² (asking), and both projects have already sold out.

### How I handle permits

Permits are what separate a project from a picture. No stage will start before there is a right to carry it out. The building permit comes first: home sales open after the building permit, and home prices will be published once the site and building permit are confirmed.

At the end comes the occupancy permit, followed by the handover of the homes.

### How I link the money to the construction

Financing: founders' capital, a bank development loan and a small group of private co-investors. Home sales open after the building permit. My job is to make sure every euro goes into a stage that will be completed on site. A stage is complete when it is visible in the report, not by the calendar.

Joining the list creates no contract and no investment.

### The order of stages

Groundworks, foundations, structure, roof with panels, services, façade, interiors, pool, grounds. Nine stages. Boris Ribaut will run the site. Home buyers will see every stage in the weekly report.

Home prices will be published once the site and building permit are confirmed. Market reference: neighbouring La Quinta new builds 3,360–3,870 €/m² (asking, sold out); plus resale La Quinta 2,812 €/m² (Fotocasa, October 2026).
