# I am Simon Subic, developer of Artemis de la Cruz

I am a Development Principal, working between London and Paris. At Artemis de la Cruz, twenty residences at La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, on the northern slope of Tenerife, I lead the choice of plot, the permits, the launch and the link between the money and the construction. The construction is paid for by shares in the developer company and by house sales. Start, as planned, in early 2027. Handover, as planned, in late 2028 – mid 2029.

### What I do

I run development projects from the owner's side: from choosing the asset to the moment the building works. My chain looks like this:

**Project criteria → search for a plot → asset due diligence → investment logic → deal structure → permits → capital → development platform → construction launch → operation, refinancing or exit.**

In this chain I do three things:

- **Choosing and checking the asset.** Whether the plot fits, whether the economics hold, where the risks are.
- **Capital, permits and participants.** How to link the money, the permits and everyone who influences the project.
- **The platform and the construction launch.** The team, the control and the order without which construction never reaches the site.

I am a Slovenian citizen, working on a London platform and in France.

### Why La Quinta

The north of Tenerife is green, not hot, not dusty. From La Quinta to Puerto de la Cruz it is 8 km, 10–15 minutes by car; to Tenerife Norte airport 15–20 minutes; to Santa Cruz 25–30 minutes. People live here all year round. I build where people live, not where they spend a week.

Twenty houses: six one-bedroom of 48–55 м², ten two-bedroom of 68–78 м², four large of 85–100 м². A small, enclosed complex, not a neighbourhood of hundreds of flats. For comparison: the neighbouring new developments at La Quinta were offered at 3 360–3 870 € per metre, and both projects are already sold; a finished Artemis house at handover is 3 800–4 600 €.

### How I handle the permits

Permits separate a project from a picture. No stage begins before the right to it exists. The building licence is the first price step after the waiting list: the metre rises from 2 950 € to 3 100 € because the project has acquired the right to build. On 72 metres that is 10 800 € of difference: 212 400 € on the waiting list and 223 200 € at the permit step.

At the end comes the completion certificate. From that moment the shares can be exchanged for the metres of your own residence.

### How I link the money and the construction

The construction is financed by two flows: shares in the developer company and house sales. My job is to make every euro go into the stage that is rising on site. The price step rises when the stage is closed on site and visible in the report, not by the calendar.

Joining the waiting list is free. We are currently choosing the plot in the La Quinta area of Santa Úrsula and discussing the options with waiting-list members. We take no money before the plot is chosen: the investment agreement arrives when the majority of the waiting list approves the plot. Under the agreement you buy shares in the developer company at the price of the current step: one share is one metre. In full straight away: a 10% bonus; in instalments over six months: a 5% bonus; in instalments over a year: no bonus. Every week of construction: a report. After handover: a dividend in euros on each share from long-term letting less costs, estimated, before company profit tax, or an exchange of shares for the metres of your own residence.

### The order of stages and the price steps

Ground, foundation, shell, roof with panels, services, façade, interior works, pool, plot. Nine stages. The site is run by Boris Ribaut. The shareholder and the house buyer see every stage in the weekly report.

Six steps: waiting list 2 950 €, permit 3 100 €, foundation 3 250 €, shell and roof 3 450 €, finishing 3 650 €, handover 3 850 € per metre. The further the construction goes, the more expensive entry becomes.

**A timetable.** As planned, start in early 2027 and handover in late 2028 – mid 2029.

**Order.** Nine stages, one after another, a report every week.

**A person.** If the report differs from what you see on site, you speak to me. I find out why and answer you.

### How we work

I lead the project: choosing the plot, permits, launch, linking the money and the construction. Boris Ribaut builds: the site, the structure, the reports, handover of the house. We make the calculations for every shareholder on one formula: metres × the step price, the payment = amount ÷ 12, the dividend = shares × euros per share. Two areas of responsibility, and no question without an owner.

### What I expect from you

Questions. The more you ask before buying shares, the calmer you will be during construction. That is what the time between joining the waiting list and the investment agreement is for: we discuss plot options in the closed group of participants, you receive a calculation for your area, check it against the calculator and put questions to me and to Boris Ribaut. Nothing has to be paid before the plot is chosen.

Ask me about the plot, the permits, the timetable, and what happens if a stage slips. I will answer directly.
