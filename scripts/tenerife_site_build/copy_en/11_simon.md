# I'm Simon Subic, developer of Artemis Tenerife

I'm a Development Principal working between London and Paris. On Artemis Tenerife — twenty residences in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, on the northern slope of Tenerife — I lead site selection, permits, launch and linking the money to the construction. The construction money comes from developer company shares and home sales. Start: planned for early 2027. Completion: planned for late 2028 to mid-2029.

[FRAME F19] <!-- internal marker, not published -->

### What I do

I run development projects on the owner's side: from choosing the asset to the moment the home is up and running. My chain looks like this:

**Project criteria → site search → asset due diligence → investment logic → deal structure → permits → capital → development platform → construction launch → operation, refinancing or exit.**

Within this chain I do three things:

- **Choosing and checking the asset.** Is the site right, does the economics hold up, where are the risks.
- **Capital, permits and stakeholders.** How to connect the money, the permits and everyone who influences the project.
- **Platform and construction launch.** The team, controls and procedures without which construction never gets onto the site.

I'm a Slovenian citizen, working on a London-based platform and in France.

### Why La Quinta

The north of Tenerife is green, not too hot, not dusty. From La Quinta it is 8 km to Puerto de la Cruz, 10–15 minutes by car; 15–20 minutes to Tenerife North airport; 25–30 minutes to Santa Cruz. People live here all year round. I build where people live, not where they spend a week.

Twenty homes: six one-bedroom homes of 48–55 m², ten two-bedroom homes of 68–78 m² and four large homes of 85–100 m². An intimate complex, not a block of hundreds of flats. For comparison, the neighbouring La Quinta new builds were offered at 3,360–3,870 € per metre, and both projects have already sold out.

### How I handle permits

Permits are what separate a project from a picture. No stage will start before there is a right to carry it out. The building licence is the first share price stage after the queue: the metre will cost more because the project will have the right to build.

At the end comes the occupancy permit. From that moment, shares can be exchanged for the metres of your own residence.

### How I link the money to the construction

Construction is funded by two streams: developer company shares and home sales. My job is to make sure every euro goes into a stage that will rise on site. The price stage will move up when a phase is completed on site and is visible in the report, not by the calendar.

Joining the queue is free. We are now choosing the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, and discussing the options with queue members. We take no money before the site is chosen: the investment contract arrives once most of the queue approves the site. Under the contract you buy developer company shares at the price of the current stage: one share equals one m² of the home's total built area, excluding common areas. Pay in full upfront for a 10% discount on the entry price, in instalments over six months — six equal payments with a 5% discount — or in instalments over a year with no discount. A report every week of construction. Once the home is completed, you receive a dividend in euros on every share from long-term rental after costs, estimated and before tax, or exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence.

### The order of stages and the price steps

Groundworks, foundations, structure, roof with panels, services, façade, interiors, pool, grounds. Nine stages. Boris Ribaut will run the site. Shareholders and home buyers will see every stage in the weekly report.

Six price stages: queue, permit, foundations, structure and roof, finishing, completion. The price per metre is ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion — an estimate based on current prices of comparable new apartments in the area. The further construction goes, the more it costs to enter.

This is a forecast, not a guarantee: the dividend may be lower or may not be paid, and the share price may fall. Tax consequences depend on your personal situation.

### How we work

I run the project: site selection, permits, launch, linking the money to the construction. Boris Ribaut will build: the site, structure, reports, handover of the homes. We work out the figures for every shareholder with one formula: entry price = metres × price per metre, dividend = shares × euros per share. You can pay in full upfront with a 10% discount on the entry price; over six months in six equal payments with no down payment, the first on signing the contract, a 5% discount on the whole price, each payment = entry price × 0.95 ÷ 6; or over a year with no discount, in which case the monthly payment = entry price ÷ 12. Two areas of responsibility, and no question without an owner.

### What I expect from you

Questions. The more you ask before buying shares, the calmer you will be during construction. That is what the time between joining the queue and the investment contract is for: we discuss the site options in a private group of members, you receive a calculation for your area and ask Boris Ribaut and me your questions. You don't need to pay anything before the site is chosen.

Ask me about the site, permits, timelines and what happens if a stage slips. I will give you a straight answer.
