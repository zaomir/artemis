# Construction planned for 2027–2029: nine stages and a weekly report

Artemis Tenerife will break ground once the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, has been chosen and the building permit granted, planned for early 2027. Nine stages, from groundworks and foundations to the pool and grounds. Completion is planned for late 2028 to mid-2029. Each stage will move the developer company share price up a step, and every week shareholders and home buyers will receive a report.

### The site log

The log will be kept in one format: **date → stage → what was done**. Entries will begin when work starts on site. Until then, the log holds only the key project dates, in order and as planned:

| Date (planned) | Milestone | What will happen |
|---|---|---|
| early 2027 | Construction starts | work begins on site in La Quinta |
| late 2028 – mid-2029 | Completion | occupancy permit; your choice: dividend or metres |

Once work starts, only completed steps will go into the log, and every entry will appear in the weekly report. A rendering will never appear in the log — only what has been done on site.

### What you will receive every week

**A construction report.** Which stage is under way, what was completed that week, what comes next and when the next price stage is due.

**A report for everyone.** Shareholders and home buyers will receive the report, from their first purchase to the keys.

You won't wait two years for your keys in the dark. Every week you will see your money turning into concrete, roof and glass.

### Who will run the site

The site will be run by chief builder **Boris Ribaut**: the site, structure, reports, handover of the homes. Site selection, permits, launch and linking the money to the construction are the job of developer **Simon Subic**.

If anything in a report is unclear, or it doesn't match what you see on site, you know whom to write to. And a person answers, not a department.

**Contractor.** We will choose the main contractor after the design and the building permit, through an open tender among vetted local companies: the best price, the right quality, the right timeline. We have no favourite contractor. We will agree the choice with investors who have voting rights.

**Cameras.** We will install video cameras on site and pass the weekly reports from the designer and contractors on to investors.

### The stage and your money

Joining the queue is free. We are now choosing the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, and discussing the options with queue members. We take no money before the site is chosen: the investment contract arrives once most of the queue approves the site. Under the contract you buy developer company shares at the price of the current stage: one share equals one m² of the home's total built area, excluding common areas. Pay in full upfront for a 10% discount on the entry price, in instalments over six months — six equal payments with a 5% discount — or in instalments over a year with no discount. A report every week of construction. Once the home is completed, you receive a dividend in euros on every share from long-term rental after costs, estimated and before tax, or exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence.

The further construction goes, the more it costs to enter. Here is what completes each stage:

- **Permit**: the building licence.
- **Foundations**: the base and the stone plinth.
- **Structure and roof**: walls, floors, roof with panels.
- **Finishing**: façade, services, interiors, pool.
- **Completion**: the occupancy permit.

Each of these milestones is an entry in the log and a line in the weekly report. The stage will move up when the phase is completed on site and this is visible in the report, not by the calendar: the planned date is a guide, the price is tied to the event. A metre as a share costs ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion — an estimate based on current prices of comparable new apartments in the area. Before construction, investors' money covers the team, design and permits. Construction is paid for by developer company shares and home sales.

This is a forecast, not a guarantee: the dividend may be lower or may not be paid, and the share price may fall. Tax consequences depend on your personal situation.

### What you get at completion

A new build, not someone else's renovation. A stone shell, a roof with solar panels, smart climate and lighting, deep windows and a shared heated pool.
