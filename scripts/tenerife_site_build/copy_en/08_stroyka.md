# Construction planned for 2027–2029: nine stages and a weekly report

Artemis Tenerife will break ground once the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, has been chosen and the building permit granted, planned for early 2027. Nine stages, from groundworks and foundations to the pool and grounds. Completion is planned for late 2028 to mid-2029. Every week home buyers will receive a report.

| Construction stage | What completes the stage |

### The site log

The log will be kept in one format: **date → stage → what was done**. Entries will begin when work starts on site. Until then, the log holds only the key project dates, in order and as planned:

| Date (planned) | Milestone | What will happen |
|---|---|---|
| early 2027 | Construction starts | work begins on site in La Quinta |
| late 2028 – mid-2029 | Completion | occupancy permit; homes handed over |

Once work starts, only completed steps will go into the log, and every entry will appear in the weekly report. A rendering will never appear in the log — only what has been done on site.

### Who will run the site

The site will be run by chief builder **Boris Ribaut**: the site, structure, reports, handover of the homes. Site selection, permits, launch and linking the money to the construction are the job of developer **Simon Subic**.

If anything in a report is unclear, or it doesn't match what you see on site, you know whom to write to. And a person answers, not a department.

**Contractor.** We will choose the main contractor after the design and the building permit, through an open tender among vetted local companies: the best price, the right quality, the right timeline. We have no favourite contractor.

**Cameras.** We will install video cameras on site and pass the weekly reports from the designer and contractors on to home buyers.

### What we will build with

- **Dark volcanic stone** in the plinth: the island's own material, heavy and long-lasting.
- **Lime render**: a light, warm façade that ages gracefully in the sun.
- **A flat roof** with a slim bronze edge and dark solar panels flush with the roof.
- **Tall windows** in slim bronze frames, set deep into the wall.
- **Stone terraces**, bronze handrails and glass.

One architectural language for all twenty homes: one technology, one team, one standard from the first home to the twentieth.

### Financing and the stages

20 residences planned in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula. Concept stage: we are selecting the site. Financing: founders' capital, a bank development loan and a small group of private co-investors. Home sales open after the building permit.

Here is what completes each milestone:

- **Permit**: the building licence.
- **Foundations**: the base and the stone plinth.
- **Structure and roof**: walls, floors, roof with panels.
- **Finishing**: façade, services, interiors, pool.
- **Completion**: the occupancy permit.

Each of these milestones is an entry in the log and a line in the weekly report. A milestone is reached when the phase is completed on site and this is visible in the report, not by the calendar: the planned date is a guide.

Home prices will be published once the site and building permit are confirmed. Market reference: neighbouring La Quinta new builds 3,360–3,870 €/m² (asking, sold out); plus resale La Quinta 2,812 €/m² (Fotocasa, October 2026).

### What you get at completion

A new build, not someone else's renovation. A stone shell, a roof with solar panels, smart climate and lighting, deep windows and a shared heated pool.
