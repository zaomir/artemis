Homes from 48 to 100 m² — three layouts, one architectural language

For a winter together, a family move or a home with room for guests. The plan comprises six one-bedroom residences, ten two-bedroom homes and four larger homes in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula.

One bedroom · 48–55 m²

For one person or a couple. An open kitchen and living room, a separate bedroom, bathroom and terrace beyond the glass. A manageable size for winter stays and remote work.

Two bedrooms · 68–78 m²

A second bedroom for children, guests or a study. The living room opens onto the terrace; deep-set windows bring light and shade at different times of day.

The layout separates shared and private space, so guests can stay while another room is used for work or rest.

Large home · 85–100 m²

More room for family, guests, work and longer stays. Four such residences in the proposed development.

Project specification

Lime plaster, a volcanic-stone plinth, tall bronze-framed windows and stone terraces. Roof-mounted solar panels, smart climate and lighting inside. A shared heated pool designed for year-round use.

Paying for a home

Proposed buyer schedule: 40% at signing, another 30% at foundations, 10% at shell completion and the final 20% at settlement. Buyers signing later pay completed milestones at signing.

You purchase your chosen residence and receive the keys on completion. A separate investment calculation covers participation with a cash exit. Investors · Process.
