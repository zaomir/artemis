Twenty residences from 48 to 100 metres, one Canary Stone façade

Twenty Artemis residences in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula: six one-bedroom homes of 48–55 m², ten two-bedroom homes of 68–78 m² and four large homes of 85–100 m². A metre bought as a developer company share costs ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion: one share equals one square metre of the home's total built area, excluding common areas.

One bedroom · 48–55 m² · 6 of 20

This residence = 48–55 developer company shares.

For one person or a couple. For anyone who spends the winter in the north, works from home or comes to their home a few times a year. An open-plan room where living room and kitchen share one space, a bedroom, a bathroom and a terrace behind glass.

Nothing superfluous here: everything you need and nothing you would pay for without using. A small home is easier to look after, easier to lock up for a month and easier to let long term.

In figures. 48–55 metres means 48–55 shares. Entry price = metres × price per metre, ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion. After completion: 48–55 metres of your own home, or a dividend on 48–55 shares; the expected dividend is 2.5–3.5% a year before tax at the queue entry price. If you take the keys and let it yourself, long-term rent at market rates of 12–15 € per metre comes to 576–825 € a month.

This is a forecast, not a guarantee: the dividend may be lower or may not be paid, and the share price may fall. Tax consequences depend on your personal situation.

Two bedrooms · 68–78 m² · 10 of 20

This residence = 68–78 developer company shares.

Half the complex. The second bedroom becomes a room for children, guests or a study. The living room opens onto a stone terrace, and light comes in through tall bronze-framed windows set deep into the wall: in summer the reveal keeps the shade, in winter it lets in the low sun.

This is a home for a family moving to the north, and for anyone who lives here half the year and lets it the rest of the time. On a long-term let in the north, 72 metres at market rates of 12–15 € per metre bring in 864–1,080 € a month.

In figures. 68–78 metres means 68–78 shares. Entry price = metres × price per metre, ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion. After completion: your own home or a dividend; the expected dividend is 2.5–3.5% a year before tax at the queue entry price.

Large home · 85–100 m² · 4 of 20

This residence = 85–100 developer company shares.

Four homes in the whole complex. For those who come to the north seriously and for the long term: more rooms, more terrace, more light, space for guests and for work. A home you stay in, not one you just holiday in.

In figures. 85–100 metres means 85–100 shares. Entry price = metres × price per metre, ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion. After completion: your own home or a dividend on 85–100 shares; the expected dividend is 2.5–3.5% a year before tax at the queue entry price. Long-term rent for a home like this at market rates of 12–15 € per metre is 1,020–1,500 € a month.

What all twenty share

Outside: Canary Stone.

  • Warm lime render: a light façade that ages gracefully in the sun.
  • A plinth of dark volcanic stone: the island's own material.
  • A flat roof with a slim bronze edge and dark solar panels set flush into the roof.
  • Tall windows in slim bronze frames.
  • Stone terraces with bronze handrails and glass.

Inside. An open-plan room opening onto the terrace, light walls, stone and bronze details. Smart climate and lighting. Deep windows and a stone shell reduce heat loss.

A shared heated pool. Evenings in the north can be cool, and heating keeps the pool usable all year round.

A home or shares

A home. You can also buy the finished new build at completion; the price depends on the type, floor and view. Our brokers help home buyers get a mortgage from banks in Spain and other European countries. A mortgage is available only to a buyer, but an investor can pay a first instalment of 50% from their own funds and cover the rest with a mortgage once the building permit is granted.

Developer company shares. Joining the queue is free. We are now choosing the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, and discussing the options with queue members. We take no money before the site is chosen: the investment contract arrives once most of the queue approves the site. Under the contract you buy developer company shares at the price of the current stage: one share equals one m² of the home's total built area, excluding common areas. Pay in full upfront for a 10% discount on the entry price, in instalments over six months — six equal payments with a 5% discount — or in instalments over a year with no discount. A report every week of construction. Once the home is completed, you receive a dividend in euros on every share from long-term rental after costs, estimated and before tax, or exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence.

In short

How many residences?

Twenty: 6 + 10 + 4.

How many shares in a residence?

The same as the number of metres: 48–55, 68–78 or 85–100.

How much is a metre as a share?

≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion — an estimate based on current prices of comparable new apartments in the area.

Free. No money before the site is chosen.
