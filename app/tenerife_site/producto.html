Twenty residences from 48 to 100 metres, one Canary Stone façade

20 residences planned in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula. Concept stage: we are selecting the site. Six one-bedroom homes of 48–55 m², ten two-bedroom homes of 68–78 m² and four large homes of 85–100 m².

1,308–1,510 m² in total across the twenty residences. An intimate complex, not a block of hundreds of flats.

Home prices will be published once the site and building permit are confirmed. Market reference: neighbouring La Quinta new builds 3,360–3,870 €/m² (asking, sold out); plus resale La Quinta 2,812 €/m² (Fotocasa, October 2026).

One bedroom · 48–55 m² · 6 of 20

For one person or a couple. For anyone who spends the winter in the north, works from home or comes to their home a few times a year. An open-plan room where living room and kitchen share one space, a bedroom, a bathroom and a terrace behind glass.

Nothing superfluous here: everything you need and nothing you would pay for without using. A small home is easier to look after and easier to lock up for a month.

Two bedrooms · 68–78 m² · 10 of 20

Half the complex. The second bedroom becomes a room for children, guests or a study. The living room opens onto a stone terrace, and light comes in through tall bronze-framed windows set deep into the wall: in summer the reveal keeps the shade, in winter it lets in the low sun.

This is a home for a family moving to the north, and for anyone who lives here for part of the year.

Large home · 85–100 m² · 4 of 20

Four homes in the whole complex. For those who come to the north seriously and for the long term: more rooms, more terrace, more light, space for guests and for work. A home you stay in, not one you just holiday in.

What all twenty share

Outside: Canary Stone.

  • Warm lime render: a light façade that ages gracefully in the sun.
  • A plinth of dark volcanic stone: the island's own material.
  • A flat roof with a slim bronze edge and dark solar panels set flush into the roof.
  • Tall windows in slim bronze frames.
  • Stone terraces with bronze handrails and glass.

Inside. An open-plan room opening onto the terrace, light walls, stone and bronze details. Smart climate and lighting. Deep windows and a stone shell reduce heat loss.

A shared heated pool. Evenings in the north can be cool, and heating keeps the pool usable all year round.

Buying a home

Home prices will be published once the site and building permit are confirmed. Market reference: neighbouring La Quinta new builds 3,360–3,870 €/m² (asking, sold out); plus resale La Quinta 2,812 €/m² (Fotocasa, October 2026). Home sales open after the building permit.

Financing: founders' capital, a bank development loan and a small group of private co-investors. Co-investment starts from €200,000, individually. Details are discussed in person after a call.
