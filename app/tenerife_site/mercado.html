Where the entry price and future sales revenue come from

The calculation uses a 3 360 €/m² reference, a 2 352 € early price and 6% annual growth for unsold homes. These are financial-model inputs; a current comparable local new-build price list has not yet been verified.

Early entry 30% below the reference

3 360 × 70% = 2 352 €. The difference is 30% of the comparison reference. The share price buys participation; the per-m² reference supports comparison and sales calculations, not a promise of a specific home for shares.

Before describing entry as 30% below today's market, the comparable new-build sample needs updating for area definitions, condition, specification and listing date.

What neighbouring projects show

Reside Tenerife marks La Quinta I, II, III and Lomo Román as sold. It publishes no current price list there. Older advertised prices are therefore not presented as today's market valuation or a promised resale price.

Budget references

Local practice Urbara publishes Tenerife building-cost references by specification level. The base scenario uses 1 750 €/m² of built area; land, design, shared areas and contingency are separate. There is no internal project cost plan yet.

At 2 100 €/m² the target buyback is not fully covered. Architecture and specification therefore need a confirmed cost plan before funding starts. Budget and sensitivity.

What this means for you

Early price, future house-price growth and investor return are three separate inputs. 30% sets entry against the reference; 6% drives the sales plan; 9% is the investor's target. They are not added together. Calculate an investment.
