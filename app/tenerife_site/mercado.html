Artemis next to the market

Home prices will be published once the site and building permit are confirmed. Market reference: neighbouring La Quinta new builds 3,360–3,870 €/m² (asking, sold out); plus resale La Quinta 2,812 €/m² (Fotocasa, October 2026).

How to read these figures

Asking prices are what sellers requested, not necessarily what buyers paid. Resale figures are averages over homes of different ages, sizes and condition, so they are a reference point, not a price list for Artemis homes.
