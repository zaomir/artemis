What to understand before participating

Key questions about money, timing and results. The base investment model has one full upfront payment and one cash exit after 36 months.

Capital and return

Are there annual payments?

No. Capital and income are paid on sale or buyback at exit.

What does 9% annualised mean?

A target effective return with compounding. Three years means 29,5% above investment: 169 344 € → 219 305 € for 72 shares.

Is that result guaranteed?

It is the base scenario's target. Payment depends on actual sales receipts, the budget and exit timing.

Can investors pay in instalments?

The model requires full upfront investment. The 40/30/10/20% milestones apply to homebuyers.

Price and growth

Where does the 2 352 € early price come from?

It is 70% of the provisional 3 360 €/m² reference, or 30% below it. Current comparable local new-build prices still need confirmation.

What does 6% growth mean?

An assumption for annual changes in unsold homes' prices. A signed contract fixes the price; growth is neither added retrospectively nor added to the investor's return.

Construction and funding

Who funds construction?

The early investment pool and payments from buyers of twenty homes. The model includes no additional developer capital or bank debt.

Is the base scenario fully funded?

Its monthly schedule covers expenses and the target exit, with a minimum cash balance of 53 083 €. That calculation assumes sales and payments arrive on schedule.

What if sales are delayed or costs rise?

The whole cash calendar needs recalculation. A two-month sales delay creates a 202 236 € gap; at 2 100 €/m² construction cost the target buyback is not fully covered. No automatic developer top-up is modeled.

What if the project stops?

Return timing and amount will depend on the project's position, available cash and asset sales. The model does not fund an immediate return of all capital and income. Discuss the response before payment.

Homes and investment

Do shares reserve a home?

No. Here share quantity defines an investment with a cash exit. A specific residence is purchased separately.

Can I receive a home and a buyback for the same investment?

The model does not provide that outcome: a residence cannot be sold to a buyer and transferred to an investor at the same time.

Getting started

When do I pay?

After approval of the plot and participation terms. Requesting a calculation is free.

Who answers questions?

Simon Subic and Boris Ribaut. Participation process.
