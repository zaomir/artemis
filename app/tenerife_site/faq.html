Questions before entering Artemis de la Cruz, and direct answers

These are the questions people ask before buying a house in Tenerife and before buying shares in the developer company Artemis. The answers are short, with the numbers, the same ones we give out loud at a meeting.

Location

Where is the project?

In the La Quinta area of Santa Úrsula. We choose the actual plot with waiting-list members.

How do I get there?

Routes, walking access to the beach and surroundings are on the location page.

What about winter?

Climate, the solar roof and heated pool are covered under energy and specifications.

What I am buying

What is a share?

Participation in the developer company: one share corresponds to one m² construida excluding common areas.

How does participation differ from buying a house?

The asset purchased and two participation outcomes are compared on the investor page.

Price and early entry

How does the price change?

By construction events rather than dates. Price ladder and example.

How do I calculate payments?

Choose your area and step in the calculator.

When do I pay?

After the majority of the waiting list approves the plot and the investment agreement. Documents and payment timing.

The dividend

What dividend?

An estimated 2,5–3,5 % a year before company profit tax, not guaranteed.

What is the estimate based on?

Long-term letting after costs. Rental market, costs and assumptions.

Metres

How do I receive a residence?

After the completion certificate, the exchange is formalised before a notary and in the register. Formalities, taxes and mortgage.

Can I buy more shares?

At the current step price; the investor page contains a top-up example.

Is assignment possible before handover?

If buyers outnumber units, the company may offer an assignment. It is an opportunity, not a promise of return; see the transaction details.

Construction and reports

When are construction and handover?

The plan is a start in early 2027 and handover in late 2028 – mid 2029.

Who receives reports?

Shareholders and house buyers, every week. Stages and report format.

People

Who is responsible?

Simon Subic’s and Boris Ribaut’s roles are in the team section.

Which company?

OXFORD FRAME REAL ASSETS LTD. Company details.

What homes?

Residence types, areas and specifications.

In short

What should I do now?

Request a calculation and discuss plot options. Joining the waiting list is free; we take no money before the plot is chosen.
