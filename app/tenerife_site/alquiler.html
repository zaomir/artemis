A home to use in your own way

Live year-round, spend winters here or let it long-term — the residence owner decides. The Artemis investment model ends in a cash exit and does not provide annual rental distributions.

For living

A bright living room, terrace, smart climate and heated pool. Choose your size: 48 to 100 m².

For long-term letting

Future tenants will compare the layout, running costs and access to town. Before buying, check current listings for equivalent homes and allow for vacancy, maintenance and repairs. Rent collected is different from an owner's income after expenses.

For investors

The new model does not retain a rental portfolio for annual distributions. The investor pays in full and receives cash on sale or buyback at exit. The target is 9% annualised over 36 months. Investment model.

Request a calculation

Leave your name and preferred contact. Receive a personal calculation covering the investment, target income, exit period from full payment and payment source. We will discuss the sales timetable and inputs to confirm before payment.
