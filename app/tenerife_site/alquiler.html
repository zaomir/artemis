How a home in La Quinta earns after completion

Long-term rent in Santa Úrsula is 12–15 € per metre a month; in neighbouring Puerto de la Cruz it is about 17 €. For a 72-metre home, that is 864–1,080 € a month. The same long-term rental produces the dividend on Artemis developer company shares: at the queue entry price, an expected 2.5–3.5% a year before tax.

Two kinds of owner, two kinds of income

The keyholder. Bought a home or exchanged shares for metres, and lets it out themselves. Their income is the rent minus their own costs.

The shareholder. Kept their shares in the company. The company lets the metres that were not exchanged on long-term leases, and the shareholder receives a dividend in euros on every share. They don't have to find a tenant.

From rent to dividend

The dividend is calculated only from long-term rental. The formula is simple:

rent per metre per month × 12 − costs = annual rent per metre after costs = dividend per share before tax.

Step by step:

  • rent: the middle of the Santa Úrsula range, 13.5 € per metre a month;
  • per year: twelve months of rent;
  • costs across seven items: 46.3% of rent;
  • what remains: an expected dividend of 2.5–3.5% a year before tax at the queue entry price.

This is a forecast, not a guarantee: the dividend may be lower or may not be paid, and the share price may fall. Tax consequences depend on your personal situation.

The lower the costs, the more is left for the dividend.

Why long-term, not holiday lets

Under Canary Islands law Ley 6/2025, new homes cannot be used for short-term holiday lets for ten years. So the Artemis dividend is built on long-term rental: a lease of a year or more. A tenant on a lease pays every month, not from one booking to the next.

Three ways to live with your home

Live in it yourself. The simplest way to earn from a home is not to pay for someone else's. You live in La Quinta for a season or a year.

Let it long term. A lease of a year or more. In the north, this is the calmest option: people come here to live, not for a week at the beach.

Let it for a season. A lease of a few months, for people who winter on the island or work from home.

Who rents in the north

The north is rented by people who want greenery and a town, not sunbeds: Puerto de la Cruz is 8 km away, Tenerife North airport 15–20 minutes, Santa Cruz 25–30 minutes. They need a new home that is not cold in winter and not stuffy in summer.

How you enter

Joining the queue is free. We are now choosing the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, and discussing the options with queue members. We take no money before the site is chosen: the investment contract arrives once most of the queue approves the site. Under the contract you buy developer company shares at the price of the current stage: one share equals one m² of the home's total built area, excluding common areas. Pay in full upfront for a 10% discount on the entry price, in instalments over six months — six equal payments with a 5% discount — or in instalments over a year with no discount. A report every week of construction. Once the home is completed, you receive a dividend in euros on every share from long-term rental after costs, estimated and before tax, or exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence.
