How participation works: six steps from enquiry to exit

Start with the project and a calculation. Payment follows approval of the plot and participation terms. Investors pay the entire amount once; the base model's cash exit is after 36 months.

Six steps

  1. 1

    Enquiry.

    Leave your name and contact. Free, with no commitment.

  2. 2

    Personal calculation.

    Receive the entry amount, target result, term and assumptions. For 72 shares the example is 169 344 € → 219 305 €.

  3. 3

    Plot and terms.

    Before payment, review the selected asset, budget, sales calendar and cash-exit process, including timetable changes.

  4. 4

    Full payment.

    Pay the entire investment in one payment. The 36-month model period starts on this date.

  5. 5

    Construction and sales.

    Weekly reports on work and cash are planned. Investment capital and buyer payments fund expenses.

  6. 6

    Cash exit.

    Sale or buyback of the participation funded by home-sale receipts. Capital and income are paid at exit; there are no annual distributions.

If you are buying a home

Choose a specific residence. Proposed payments: 40% at signing, 30% at foundations, 10% at shell completion and 20% at final settlement. Late buyers catch up completed milestones immediately. The price is locked at signing.

A home purchase does not simultaneously include a cash buyback of the same investment. Residence catalogue.
