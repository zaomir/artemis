I am Simon Subic, developer of Artemis de la Cruz

I am a Development Principal working between London and Paris. At Artemis de la Cruz I lead plot selection, project preparation and funding for twenty residences. I connect initial investment and buyer payments with the work programme.

What I do

I run development projects from the owner's side: from choosing the asset to the moment the building works. My chain looks like this:

Project criteria → search for a plot → asset due diligence → investment logic → deal structure → permits → capital → development platform → construction launch → operation, refinancing or exit.

In this chain I do three things:

  • Choosing and checking the asset.Whether the plot fits, whether the economics hold, where the risks are.
  • Capital, permits and participants.How to link the money, the permits and everyone who influences the project.
  • The platform and the construction launch.The team, the control and the order without which construction never reaches the site.

I am a Slovenian citizen, working on a London platform and in France.

Why La Quinta

The north of Tenerife is green, not hot, not dusty. From La Quinta to Puerto de la Cruz it is 8 km, 10–15 minutes by car; to Tenerife Norte airport 15–20 minutes; to Santa Cruz 25–30 minutes. People live here all year round. I build where people live, not where they spend a week.

Twenty homes: six one-bedroom residences of 48–55 m², ten two-bedroom homes of 68–78 m² and four larger homes of 85–100 m². A small development with a shared architectural language and infrastructure. Residences and purchase calculations.

How I handle the permits

I coordinate documents and sequencing with designers. Project preparation follows the work and spending programme; completion of construction stages is recorded on site and in reports.

How I link the money and the construction

Construction has two sources: the initial investment pool and homebuyer payments. I link each stage to available cash and the sales calendar. Full investor payment and buyer milestone payments are separate streams.

Stages and participation calculation

The nine stages, planned dates and weekly report format are in Project progress.

Early price 2 352 € per share, full payment and target cash exit after 36 months. Investor model.

What you get from my work

Schedule. The financial model allows 36 months to exit. Construction dates will follow plot selection.

Order. Nine stages, one after another, a report every week.

A person. If the report differs from what you see on site, you speak to me. I find out why and answer you.

How we work

I lead the plot, preparation and funding. Boris Ribaut oversees the site, structure and reports. Investor calculation: shares × early price; target exit = full investment × (1 + 9%)³. Homebuyers receive a separate 40/30/10/20% schedule.
