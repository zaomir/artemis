Invest in construction — receive capital and income at exit

Participation in Artemis de la Cruz starts at 112 896 €. Pay in full at entry, target 9% annualised and exit in cash after 36 months through a sale or buyback of the participation. There are no annual distributions. Calculate your amount.

Project and current stage

Twenty residences in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula. The plot is still being selected. You can request a free calculation and discuss the terms now; payment follows approval of the plot and participation terms. The financial period starts at full payment, not at enquiry.

A share is a unit of participation. Its price is compared with a reference per m², but a share quantity does not reserve a specific home. Cash exits and residence purchases are calculated separately.

Early price and comparison reference

The model’s early price is 2 352 € per share: 70% of the provisional 3 360 €/m² reference, or 30% below it.

For unsold homes, the model assumes 6% annual growth. Each buyer's price is fixed at signing and does not rise afterwards. Reference, formulas and sources.

Cash exit after 36 months

For 72 shares: full investment 169 344 €, target exit proceeds 219 305 €, including income of 49 961 €. The total increase is 29,5% over three years, equivalent to 9% annualised with compounding.

Base-scenario formula: exit proceeds = investment × (1 + 9%)³.

How construction is funded

Two sources: the initial investment pool and payments from buyers of twenty homes. The base funding plan includes no additional developer contribution or bank debt.

Buyers pay in stages of 40/30/10/20%. Buyers signing later pay completed milestones immediately. The planned monthly cash calendar links these receipts with construction spending and investor settlement.

Returns and taxes

Returns are not guaranteed. 9% annualised and an exit after 36 months are base-scenario targets. The amount and timing of capital and income payments depend on actual project sales and costs.

We do not calculate each investor’s individual taxes; website figures are before those taxes. Before participating, obtain independent advice from a lawyer and an accountant.
