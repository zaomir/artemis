For Artemis Tenerife investors: no money before the site is chosen

Artemis Tenerife is now choosing a plot of land and building its queue. You pay nothing until we have found the best site and most queue members have approved it.

Below is the whole investor journey, step by step: from joining the queue to exchanging your shares for square metres.

What matters up front

Putting money into a home that doesn't exist yet takes trust. Before you do, you want to know where it will be built, who will build it and how you will see what is happening on site.

Without those answers, money goes in blind. That is why our order is different: first a site approved by queue members, then the contract and the money, then construction you will see every week.

The investor journey

Site and queue. We are now choosing a plot of land and building the queue. With queue members we discuss the site options, share news and listen to your wishes. We will be glad of, and grateful for, your feedback.

Choosing the site. We find the best site and put it to queue members for approval. The site is chosen once the majority has approved it. Until then, we take no money.

Contract and shares. Once the site is chosen, we send investment contracts to all queue members. Under the contract you buy shares: in full upfront with a 10% discount on the entry price, in instalments over six months — six equal payments with a 5% discount — or in instalments over a year with no discount. A sale and purchase agreement cannot be signed at this stage: there is no project documentation yet and the square metres have not been physically allocated.

Design and permits. Investors' money covers the team, design and permits. The team obtains permits from the local authorities and oversees the designers.

Contractor and construction. After the design and the building permit, we will choose a local main contractor through an open tender: the best price, the right quality, the right timeline. We have no favourite contractor — we have vetted local contractors, and we agree the choice with investors who have voting rights. During construction, our development team will control quality, install video cameras on site and share the designer's and contractors' weekly reports with you.

Metres and buyers. After completion, you have the right to convert your shares into square metres: 1 share = 1 m² of the home's total built area, excluding common areas. Once the project has the right to sell, not just to raise investment, we sign sale and purchase agreements with buyers, including with a mortgage: our brokers help them get one from banks in Spain and other European countries. If there turn out to be more buyers than units, we will offer investors the chance to pass their unit to a buyer at the price per square metre in effect at that moment.

We don't need your money before the site is chosen

Joining the queue is free. The form asks only for your name, contact details and preferred language.

You pay nothing until we have found the best site and received approval from most queue members. The investment contract arrives only after the site is chosen.

What changes in the queue

  • Now:you see the site options and tell us what you think of them.
  • After the site is chosen:you receive the investment contract and decide whether to sign it.
  • During construction:you receive the designer's and contractors' weekly reports.
  • After completion:you can convert your shares into square metres.

What do I pay when I join? Nothing.

When does the contract arrive? Once we have found the site and most queue members have approved it.

Why not a deed of sale? There is no project documentation yet and the square metres have not been physically allocated. So at the stage after the site is chosen, only an investment contract can be signed.

How do I pay? Choose the option that suits you:

  • in full upfront: a 10% discount on the entry price;
  • in instalments over six months: six equal payments with no down payment, the first on signing the contract; a 5% discount on the whole price;
  • in instalments over a year: no discount.

Can I get a mortgage? A mortgage is available only to a buyer, but an investor can pay a first instalment of 50% from their own funds and cover the rest with a mortgage once the building permit is granted. Our brokers help you get one from banks in Spain and other European countries.

Private group

Queue members are invited to a private Facebook group. There we discuss the site options, share project news and gather your wishes.

Your voice is heard before any contracts or money. Ask questions and make suggestions — we will be grateful for your feedback.
