La Quinta, Santa Úrsula: the north of Tenerife, where people live

La Quinta is a residential district of Santa Úrsula on the green northern slope of Tenerife. Puerto de la Cruz is 8 km away, 10–15 minutes by car; Tenerife Norte airport is 15–20 minutes away; Playa del Ancón is about 3 km away. This is where we are choosing the plot for twenty Artemis residences. Shares in the developer company on the waiting list are 2 950 € per metre.

Where it is on the map

Santa Úrsula lies between Puerto de la Cruz and the island's capital. La Quinta is its coastal part below the TF-5, on a slope that faces the Atlantic. Exit 29 leads off the TF-5 to the district.

North against south

The south is beach, heat, yellow dust and a holiday flow that changes every week. People fly there to rest.

The north is greenery, a mild day without heat, banana terraces, laurel forest on the slopes and Mount Teide above the clouds. People come here to live: the town is close, the capital is close, the airport is close. The surroundings are Western European.

If you have already looked at the south, you know what January looks like there: the same sun loungers and the same people with suitcases. In the north, January is a green slope, a “sea of clouds” above it at around 800–1 500 metres, and warm water in the heated pool in the evening.

  • The beaches of Santa Úrsula.Playa del Ancón, and along the coastal path Los Patos and Bollullo.

  • La Orotava.A historic town and its valley next door.

La Quinta as a district

La Quinta is a residential area, not a holiday zone. People live here all year round; they do not check in for a week.

For a house this means two things. The neighbours are people who live here permanently and do not change every week. And the long-term letting from which an Artemis shareholder's dividend is calculated rests on those who need the town, the capital and the airport close by: residents, not the tourist season.

The twenty Artemis houses form a small, enclosed complex, not a tower block. One architectural language, Canary Stone: warm lime plaster, a plinth of dark volcanic stone, flat roofs with solar panels, tall windows in bronze frames, stone terraces.

Why prices in the north are rising

The north has stopped being “the side of the island people go to on an excursion”. Flats in Santa Úrsula, according to Engel & Völkers, rose by 14,27% in 2024 and by 17,72% in 2025. All housing in Santa Úrsula, according to Fotocasa, is +13,7% over the past year.

Letting in the north

Long-term letting in Santa Úrsula is about 12 € per metre a month according to Fotocasa, and flats according to Engel & Völkers are 15,04 €. In Puerto de la Cruz it is about 17 €. The gross rental yield in Santa Úrsula, according to Mesurae, is 5,8%. For a house of 72 metres at 12–15 € per metre that is 864–1 080 € a month. The Artemis shareholder's dividend is calculated from exactly this long-term letting.

How entry works

Joining the waiting list is free. We are currently choosing the plot in the La Quinta area of Santa Úrsula and discussing the options with waiting-list members. We take no money before the plot is chosen: the investment agreement arrives when the majority of the waiting list approves the plot. Under the agreement you buy shares in the developer company at the price of the current step: one share is one metre. In full straight away: a 10% bonus; in instalments over six months: a 5% bonus; in instalments over a year: no bonus. Every week of construction: a report. After handover: a dividend in euros on each share from long-term letting less costs, estimated, before company profit tax, or an exchange of shares for the metres of your own residence.
