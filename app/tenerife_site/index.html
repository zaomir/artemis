20 residences in La Quinta: developer company shares, 1 share = 1 m²

Artemis Tenerife is twenty residences in the green north of Tenerife. We are now choosing the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula. You can come in before the keys exist, through shares in the developer company: one share equals one square metre of the home's total built area, excluding common areas. The price per metre is ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by the time the homes are completed — an estimate based on current prices of comparable new apartments in the area. Joining the queue is free, and we take no money before the site is chosen.

Location

La Quinta is a residential area of Santa Úrsula on the northern slope, between Puerto de la Cruz and the island's capital. Exit 29 off the TF-5 motorway takes you there.

Bus line 30 runs from La Quinta to Tenerife North airport — about 20 minutes, every hour. The north of Tenerife means greenery, mild days without scorching heat or dust, banana terraces and laurel forest on the slopes. Puerto de la Cruz has Loro Parque, Playa Jardín and the Lago Martiánez pools. People holiday in the south for a week. In the north, they live.

The homes

Twenty residences in one architectural language: Canary Stone. Warm lime render, a plinth of dark volcanic stone, flat roofs with dark solar panels set flush into the roof, tall windows in slim bronze frames, stone terraces with bronze handrails and glass.

Inside: an open-plan room opening onto the terrace, light through deep windows, smart climate and lighting. Deep windows and a stone shell keep you warm in winter and shaded in summer. The shared heated pool will be usable even in January. You get a new build, not someone else's renovation.

Under the contract you buy developer company shares at the price of the current stage: one share equals one m² of the home's total built area, excluding common areas. Pay in full upfront for a 10% discount on the entry price, in instalments over six months — six equal payments with a 5% discount — or in instalments over a year with no discount. A report every week of construction. Once the home is completed, you receive a dividend in euros on every share from long-term rental after costs, estimated and before tax, or exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence.

The entry price for your area, discounts and instalments are on the Terms page.

The share price is planned to rise with each construction stage: queue, permit, foundations, structure and roof, finishing, completion. After completion, the expected dividend is 2.5–3.5% a year before tax at the queue entry price. It is calculated from long-term rental of a metre minus itemised costs: IBI property tax, community fees, management, vacancy, insurance, a repair reserve and the rubbish collection charge.

This is a forecast, not a guarantee: the dividend may be lower or may not be paid, and the share price may fall. Tax consequences depend on your personal situation.

Construction

Start: planned for early 2027. Completion: planned for late 2028 to mid-2029. Nine stages: groundworks, foundations, structure, roof with panels, services, façade, interiors, pool, grounds. The site log will be kept as entries "date → stage → what was done", and a report will go out every week to shareholders and buyers.

Questions

What am I buying?

Shares in the Artemis Tenerife developer company. One share equals one square metre.

How much does it cost to enter?

≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion — an estimate based on current prices of comparable new apartments in the area.

When do I pay?

Once most of the queue has approved the site and the investment contract has arrived: in full upfront with a 10% discount on the entry price, over six months in six equal payments with a 5% discount, or over a year with no discount, monthly payment = entry price ÷ 12.

How does the dividend work?

The same amount in euros on every share, from long-term rental after costs; the expected dividend is 2.5–3.5% a year before tax at the queue entry price.

How do I exchange shares for metres?

After the occupancy permit: a deed of sale at the notary and registration in the property register.

Who will see the reports?

Shareholders and home buyers, every week.

Can I get a mortgage?

A mortgage is available only to a buyer, but an investor can pay a first instalment of 50% from their own funds and cover the rest with a mortgage once the building permit is granted. Our brokers help you get a mortgage from banks in Spain and other European countries.

Can I sell my unit before the keys?

If there turn out to be more buyers than units, we will offer investors the chance to pass their unit to a buyer at the price per square metre in effect at that moment. This is an opportunity, not a promise of income.
