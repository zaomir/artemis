Location

La Quinta is a residential district of Santa Úrsula on the northern slope, between Puerto de la Cruz and the island's capital. Exit 29 leads off the TF-5 to it.

Bus line 30 runs from La Quinta to Tenerife Norte, about 20 minutes, once an hour. The north of Tenerife is greenery, a mild day without heat or dust, banana terraces and laurel forest on the slopes. In Puerto de la Cruz there are Loro Parque, Playa Jardín and the Lago Martiánez pools. In the south people spend a week. In the north people live.

The house

Twenty residences in one architectural language, Canary Stone. Warm lime plaster, a plinth of dark volcanic stone, flat roofs with dark solar panels flush with the roof plane, tall windows in slim bronze frames, stone terraces with a bronze handrail and glass.

Inside there is an open room opening onto the terrace, light through deep windows, smart climate and lighting. The deep windows and the stone outline hold warmth in winter and shade in summer. The shared heated pool will run in January as well. What you get is a new build, not someone else's renovation.

Questions

What am I buying?

Shares in the developer company Artemis de la Cruz. One share is one square metre.

What does entry cost?

2 950 € per metre on the waiting list.

When do I pay?

After the majority of the waiting list approves the plot and the investment agreement arrives: in full (10% bonus), over six months (5% bonus) or over a year with no bonus.

How does the dividend work?

An estimated 2,5–3,5 % a year before company profit tax, from long-term letting after costs, not a guarantee.

How do I exchange shares for metres?

After the completion certificate: a deed before a notary and an entry in the register.

Who sees the reports?

Shareholders and house buyers, every week.

Can I take a mortgage?

An investor may put in 50% of their own money, and the rest can be covered by a mortgage once the building permit is in place. Our brokers help with obtaining a mortgage, at banks in Spain and in other European countries.

Can I sell my unit before handover?

If there are more buyers than units, we may offer investors the chance to assign their unit to a buyer, at the price per square metre at the time of assignment. It is an opportunity, not a promise of return.
