Location

La Quinta is a residential neighbourhood of Santa Úrsula on the northern slope, between Puerto de la Cruz and the island’s capital. Location, transport and beach access.

The homes

Twenty residences share the Canary Stone architectural language: lime render, dark volcanic stone, tall windows, terraces and a shared heated pool. Types, areas and specifications.

A residence or an investment

A residence for yourself. Compare one-bedroom, two-bedroom and larger homes in the residence catalogue.

Participation in the project. Entry at a construction-stage price and a choice after handover: retain the shares or exchange them for area. Information for investors.

Current stage

We are choosing the plot with waiting-list members. The plan is to start construction in early 2027, with handover in late 2028 – mid 2029. Project progress and weekly reporting. The sequence and payment timing are in the transaction process.
