What you receive each week

Work completed, amounts paid, receipts from buyers and the project cash balance. The log follows: date → stage → result → next step.

Watch the money become foundations, roofs and glass. Investor reporting continues through cash settlement; buyer reporting through home handover.

Who oversees the site

Boris Ribaut leads the structure, site and reporting. Simon Subic leads plot selection, launch and funding. Profiles and responsibilities.

Paying for the works

Works are funded by the initial investment pool and buyer milestone payments. Contracts fix each home’s price at signing; planned growth applies before that date. Project funding.

Weekly reports show paid works, receipts and the next stage’s budget. The calendar links construction, sales and settlement with project participants.
