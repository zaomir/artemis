Your inputs

The amount changes in 2 352 € increments, the price of one share. Calculator range: 48–100 shares, from 112 896 € to 235 200 €. The share quantity sets your participation size; it does not reserve a home.

Your results

  • Investment— one payment at entry.
  • Exit proceeds— capital and target income together.
  • Total income— proceeds less investment.
  • Target return— 9% annualised.
  • Total increase— 29,5% over three years.
  • Source of settlement— a sale or buyback funded by home-sale receipts.

Formulas

Investment = shares × 2 352 €. Exit proceeds = investment × (1 + 9%)³. Income = proceeds − investment. The 6% house-price growth assumption feeds the sales plan; it is not added to the investor's 9% return.
