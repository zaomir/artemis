Artemis Tenerife terms: price per metre, 10% and 5% discounts, instalments

Here you'll find all the terms of entry to Artemis Tenerife: what a metre costs as a share, what counts as a metre, what discounts you get for how you pay, and how to pay in instalments. We take no money before the site is approved: first the queue and the choice of site, then the investment contract and payment.

Price per metre as a share

One developer company share equals one square metre. The price per metre depends on the construction stage: ≈2,900–3,100 €/m² if you enter from the queue and ≈3,700–3,900 €/m² by completion. In between come the permit, foundations, structure and roof, and finishing: the price per metre is planned to rise with each completed stage. This is an estimate based on current prices of comparable new apartments in the area, not an exact figure.

Your entry price is your metres multiplied by the price per metre at the stage when you buy the shares. Under the investment contract, shares are sold at the price of the current stage.

What one metre means

A metre in a share is a square metre of the home's total built area — construida in Spanish — excluding common areas. The shared pool and the grounds around the homes are not part of your metres. However many metres you take, that is how many shares you buy.

The complex has twenty homes in three sizes: one bedroom, 48–55 m²; two bedrooms, 68–78 m²; large homes, 85–100 m². You can take the metres for a specific type of home, or simply the number of shares you want.

Discounts and instalments

You choose from three ways to pay:

  • In full upfront: a 10% discount on the entry price.
  • In instalments over six months: six equal payments with no down payment, the first on signing the contract; a 5% discount on the whole price.
  • In instalments over a year: no discount.

The discounts for paying upfront or over six months apply at any stage. There are no hidden factors or surcharges for your choice of payment method.

When you pay

Joining the queue is free. We are now choosing the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, and discussing the options with queue members. We take no money before the site is approved. Once most of the queue approves the site, we send you the investment contract, and payment starts only after you sign it.

Dividend or metres

Once the homes are completed, every share has two paths. You can exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence: a deed of sale at the notary and registration in the property register. Or you can keep the shares in the company: the company lets those metres on long-term leases, and you receive a dividend in euros on every share from the rent after costs.

Taxes on transactions, including when exchanging shares for metres, follow the laws of Spain and the Canary Islands; details on request.

The expected dividend is 2.5–3.5% a year before tax at the queue entry price. This is an estimate based on current long-term rents in the area.

This is a forecast, not a guarantee: the dividend may be lower or may not be paid, and the share price may fall. Tax consequences depend on your personal situation.

If you come in at a later stage

The dividend in euros per share is the same for all shares, whenever they were bought. But the price per metre is planned to be higher at each later stage. So the percentage on what you invest is lower if you enter later than if you enter from the queue.

How you enter

Joining the queue is free. We are now choosing the site in La Quinta, Santa Úrsula, and discussing the options with queue members. We take no money before the site is chosen: the investment contract arrives once most of the queue approves the site. Under the contract you buy developer company shares at the price of the current stage: one share equals one m² of the home's total built area, excluding common areas. Pay in full upfront for a 10% discount on the entry price, in instalments over six months — six equal payments with a 5% discount — or in instalments over a year with no discount. A report every week of construction. Once the home is completed, you receive a dividend in euros on every share from long-term rental after costs, estimated and before tax, or exchange your shares for the metres of your own residence.
