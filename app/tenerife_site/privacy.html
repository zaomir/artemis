Privacy policy

For the Artemis de la Cruz website and the waiting list. Version of 31.10.2026.

1. Who processes the data

Controller: OXFORD FRAME REAL ASSETS LTD, registered in England and Wales, Companies House No. 09153936. Registered office: Drovers, Preston Candover, Basingstoke, RG25 2EE. Data protection contact: info@artemisresort.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Through the waiting-list form: name, contact (email, phone or Telegram), preferred language, the page the request was sent from, date and time, and the consent record.
  • From the calculator, if the request is sent from the calculator page: the area in square metres, the price step and the payment method — in one payment or in six instalments. From these three values the server recalculates the number of shares, the price per metre, the total and the monthly payment, and stores the calculation together with the request.
  • For spam protection: a pseudonymised hash of the IP address (the IP address itself is not stored in the request database).
  • Technical logs of the web server and the CDN (IP address, time, requested page, browser) — for security and for running the site.

We do not ask for income details or payment data. You do not type an amount into the form: the calculator works it out.

3. Why and on what legal basis

To add you to the waiting list and to contact you about the Artemis de la Cruz project, including sending you a calculation from the calculator — on the basis of your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR / UK GDPR). Spam protection and technical logs — legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Who receives the data

Every request reaches the team in a Telegram working group and as a copy by email to info@artemisresort.com.

  • The hosting provider of the server (the server is located in Germany).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — CDN and site protection (data may be transferred to the United States under the standard contractual clauses / EU-US Data Privacy Framework).
  • Telegram — an internal notification to the team about a new request: name, contact, language, page and, for a calculator request, the calculation. The service is provided by Telegram Messenger Inc., a company outside the EEA, so the data is processed outside the EEA. Under the Telegram privacy policy, data is transferred within the Telegram group of companies on the basis of the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Google (Gmail, Google Workspace) — a copy of the same notification to the service address info@artemisresort.com. Google may process data outside the EEA (EU-US Data Privacy Framework / standard contractual clauses).

We do not sell the data and do not pass it to third parties for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

We keep the request together with the IP hash stored in the same record until you withdraw your consent or until the project ends. Copies of the notification in the Telegram working group and in Google Workspace mail (at the recipient info@artemisresort.com and in the sent items of the mailbox the copy goes from) are deleted together with the request: at your request or when the project ends.

Technical web server logs with IP addresses are kept for no more than 15 days and are then deleted. CDN logs are kept by Cloudflare under its own privacy policy.

6. Your rights

You may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing and portability, object to processing and withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal). You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — the ICO in the United Kingdom, or the data protection authority of your country in the EU.

7. Cookies

The site uses no analytics, no tracking tools and no advertising cookies, and the Artemis de la Cruz pages themselves write neither cookies nor localStorage into the browser. The Artemis Crete pages on the same domain set a functional cookie lang: it stores only the chosen language, lasts 1 year and belongs to the whole artemisresort.com site, so the browser sends it to the Artemis de la Cruz pages as well. Cloudflare, which provides the CDN and site protection, may set technical cookies needed for protection, for example __cf_bm and cf_clearance (description of Cloudflare cookies).
